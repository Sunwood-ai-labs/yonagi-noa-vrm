# ARDY visual QA

Captured on 2026-07-25 from the local production viewer in Google Chrome.

Coverage:

- All six ARDY motions played through the actual VRMA viewer controls.
- Each motion was captured near 25%, 50%, and 75% of its generated duration.
- All six visibly departed from the neutral A-pose.
- Lower-body weight transfer and/or stance change was visible in every motion.
- `09-talk-whisper` was additionally inspected from front, rear-oblique, and
  front-oblique camera angles because the hands approach the face and torso.
- No obvious hand-to-torso penetration, left/right hand inversion, or viewport
  clipping was observed in the inspected frames.

The two `*-timeline-contact-sheet.png` files are the compact comparison views.
The numbered PNG files are the full-size source captures.
